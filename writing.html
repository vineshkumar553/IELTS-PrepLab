Simple Habits for Better Writing

Keep these essential points in mind while practising IELTS Writing

01

Understand the Question

Read the task carefully and identify exactly what you need to describe or discuss

02

Plan Before Writing

Spend a few minutes organising your main ideas before starting your response

03

Use Clear Paragraphs

Give each paragraph one clear purpose and connect your ideas naturally

04

Check Your Answer

Leave time to review your grammar, spelling, punctuation and word count

Practice Reminder

Aim for at least 150 words in Task 1 and 250 words in Task 2
